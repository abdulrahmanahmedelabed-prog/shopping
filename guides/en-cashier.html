Payment

  • Type what the customer gave you → look at the change → Enter.
  • Card: the 💳 Pay by card on the terminal button if the terminal is connected; otherwise type the amount in the card field.
  • Wallet / banking app: the 📲 method button → the customer pays and shows you the confirmation → type the transaction number → ✓ Payment received. Don't hand over the goods before you see the confirmation.
  • On credit: choose the customer (F4), then "Credit".
  • 🎁 Offers are applied automatically. Customer points: the 🎁 Redeem points button.

Never ✋

  • ❌ Don't sell an item showing ⛔ Expired.
  • ❌ Don't take money from or put money in the drawer without a Cash withdrawal / Cash deposit.
  • ❌ Don't share your password, and never work on someone else's account.

Returns

Invoices & returns → invoice number → ↩ Return → quantity → cash or against the customer's debt. The refund uses that invoice's price, even if the price has changed.

End of shift

Cash & shifts → 🔒 Close shift → count by denomination → 🖨 Print report → hand the cash and the report to the manager.
