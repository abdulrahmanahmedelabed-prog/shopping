A day in your shop

It works with you from opening to closing

More than a till: it remembers for you, alerts you, and closes your books itself.

☀ Morning

The advisor tells you what is costing you

A best-seller about to run out, stock near expiry, a customer who stopped buying, overdue installments — each with a button that takes you to the fix.

🧾 While selling

A fast till that never stops

Barcodes and scales, offers applied automatically, every payment method, round invoice totals, and selling continues even if the network drops.

🌙 Evening

Shift close and daily summary

Count cash by denomination, shortages and overages by cashier name, and the day's summary on the owner's WhatsApp.

📅 Month end

Audit and close in one click

Financial statements, ratios, bank reconciliation and the VAT return, then lock the books so nothing changes in a closed period.

Features

Everything a shop needs in one program

Designed from day one for shops in our region: the debt book, post-dated cheques, local e-wallets, Ramadan and zakat.

🧾

Fast selling

Barcodes and scales, pieces and cartons, 3* for quantities, held invoices, a customer display, and a round total.

📲

Every payment method

Cash, card, e-wallets and banking apps by QR code and transaction number, credit, installments and other currencies. Returns at the original invoice price.

📦

Stock and expiry

Sells the nearest expiry first, stock counts by phone, smart supplier orders by WhatsApp, and a label queue for every changed price.

👥

Debts, installments and cheques

A ledger per customer with a credit limit, WhatsApp reminders, dated installments that track what is late, and cheques with due-date alerts.

💰

Real accounting

Automatic entries, balance sheet, comparative income statement and cash flow, 12 financial ratios, bank reconciliation, assets and depreciation, and month close.

🔎

A financial auditor that works on its own

Cashier fraud indicators, invoice sequence, debt aging, Benford's law, and atamper seal that exposes any change made outside the program.

🏛

E-invoicing optional

Saudi “Fatoora” phase 2 with digital signatures and phase-2 QR codes, and Jordan's JoFotara. Sent in the background.

👔

Staff and payroll

Salaries, bonuses and deductions, advances deducted automatically, a payslip for any month, and a full statement per employee.

🏬

From corner shop to chain

A simple mode for small shops, several tills on the shop network, and one report across all branches.

🛵

Online store

Customers order pickup or delivery from their phones, and an order becomes an invoice in one click. No commission to anyone.

🖨

Printing that never blocks you

Large reports are prepared in the background with a progress bar: a full year's journal in seconds while you keep selling.

🌙

Modern and comfortable

Dark and light modes, Arabic and English in under a second (item names too), and your logo on every screen and printout.

🧮

A calculator on every screen

One button or Ctrl+=, and the result goes into the field. Or type 12*3.5+4 or 100+16% inside any amount field.

🏷

Pricing calculator

From unit or carton cost and target profit to the selling price including VAT, nicely rounded — or type a price to see your profit.

📝

Notes and reminders

“Electricity every month”, “dairy order every Sunday”: a date and repeat, private or shared with staff, and an alert that never interrupts sales.

⬆

One-click update

Downloads the new version, verifies it, takes a backup, installs and reopens — your data and activation stay as they are.

Shop assistant 🧭

Ask it like you would ask your accountant

In formal Arabic, dialect or English, offline. It understands meaning, not just keywords, and answers with numbers, comparisons and tables.

Security and oversight

Your numbers belong to you

💾 On your computer

No server sees your sales or profit. A daily backup, and a second copy in Google Drive or OneDrive.

🔐 Tight permissions

Hashed passwords, every permission checked by the shop computer, and sign-in locked for a while after wrong attempts.

🧷 Tamper seal

Every invoice and every activity-log line carries a chained fingerprint; any outside change is exposed.

✋ Manager approval

For large discounts, price changes and going over a credit limit, with a full log of every action and who did it.

🏛 Ready for the financial inspector

A readiness check before an inspector or tax auditor visits, with what to do for each item.

👁 View-only inspector account

A temporary account that expires on its own; the database opens read-only for it, so nothing can be changed.

📦 Inspector file in one click

All the period's data in Excel formats and a SAF-T file, with SHA-256 fingerprints and a handover record.

📜 Access log

Everything the inspector opened, with the time, in a sealed log that cannot be deleted or edited.
